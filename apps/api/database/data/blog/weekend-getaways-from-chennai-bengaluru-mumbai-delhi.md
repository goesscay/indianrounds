A good weekend getaway is a place you can reach in a few hours and enjoy for two or three nights without much leave. From Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi there are plenty of well-known options, from beaches and hill stations to old towns and national parks. This guide groups them by starting city, with rough travel times to help you plan.

Times below are approximate and depend on your route, the season and traffic, so check current flight, train and road conditions before you book.

## How to pick a weekend destination

Start with the clock, not the map. If you leave on Friday evening and must be back for Monday morning, you have about two full days. Anything that eats more than half a day in each direction leaves little time on the ground.

Look for places with a direct flight, an overnight train or a drive of roughly three to five hours. Then think about what you want: sea air, cool hills, a little culture, or just a quiet room with a view.

## From Chennai

Chennai has good choices both by road and by air.

![Alleppey Houseboat](/photos/places/kerala/alleppey-houseboat.jpg)

- **Pondicherry** is roughly three to four hours by road, with a French-quarter feel, cafes and a calm seafront. Our [weekend in Pondicherry](/blog/a-weekend-in-pondicherry) lays out an easy plan.
- **Kerala** is reachable by a short flight or an overnight train to the coast and hills. Munnar, Alleppey and Kochi all work for a long weekend. See [Kerala](/destinations/kerala).
- **Coorg, Ooty and Kodaikanal** are hill stations that suit a road trip, though journey times can be long, so a Friday-night start helps.

## From Bengaluru

Bengaluru is a convenient base for the south and for beaches.

![Banasura Sagar Wayanad](/photos/places/kerala/banasura-sagar-wayanad.jpg)

- **Goa** is a short flight away and is the classic Bengaluru long-weekend plan. Our guide to [Goa beyond the beach](/blog/goa-beyond-the-beach-north-south-and-old-goa) covers the quieter side. See [Goa](/destinations/goa).
- **Coorg, Chikmagalur and Wayanad** are hill and coffee-country drives of a few hours, good for slow days and misty mornings.
- **Hampi and Gokarna** make a more offbeat weekend, with ruins in one and quiet beaches in the other.

## From Mumbai

Mumbai is well placed for the coast and the Western Ghats.

![City Palace Udaipur](/photos/places/rajasthan/city-palace-udaipur.jpg)

- **Goa** is a short flight or a long overnight journey by road or rail, and works well when you want sun and sea.
- **Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar and Alibaug** are within a few hours by road, good for a monsoon weekend or a quick reset.
- **Udaipur** is a short flight and offers lakes and palaces, a gentle romantic option. See [Rajasthan](/destinations/rajasthan).

## From Delhi

Delhi has the widest choice, thanks to the hills close by.

- **Uttarakhand** brings Rishikesh, Mussoorie and Nainital within a few hours to a night's journey. Our [Uttarakhand guide](/blog/uttarakhand-rishikesh-mussoorie-and-nainital-guide) helps you choose between them. See [Uttarakhand](/destinations/uttarakhand).
- **Himachal Pradesh** offers Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala, usually with an overnight road or rail journey. Read [how to choose between the Himachal towns](/blog/himachal-manali-kasol-dharamshala-how-to-choose). See [Himachal Pradesh](/destinations/himachal-pradesh).
- **Jaipur and Rajasthan** are a few hours by road or train, ideal for forts, markets and food.
- **Kashmir** is a short flight from Delhi and works for a long weekend when you want mountains and lakes. See [Kashmir](/destinations/kashmir).

## Tips for making a short trip feel longer

Small choices make a big difference on a two-night trip.

1. **Travel at night when you can.** An overnight train or a late flight turns travel time into sleep.
2. **Arrive in the morning.** A morning landing gives you a whole first day.
3. **Pick one base.** Moving hotels every night wastes hours.
4. **Book early for long weekends.** Flights, trains and stays fill up quickly around holidays.
5. **Leave room for nothing.** Keep one free evening with no plan at all.

> **Tip:** Check the return leg first. If your last-day travel gets you home late on Sunday, you may enjoy Monday far less than the weekend.

Still not sure where to go? Use mood as the guide. Beach weekends suit tired travellers who want to switch off. Hill weekends suit those who want cooler air and walks. Heritage weekends suit people who like wandering through old streets and forts.

You can also match the season: the coast is usually pleasant in the cooler months, hills are welcome in summer, and many places look their best after the rains.

Pick a city, pick a mood, and book a Friday. A good weekend is often only a few hours away.
