For a first Andaman trip, plan five to seven nights across Port Blair, Havelock and Neil Island. Fly into Port Blair, take ferries between islands, and keep the days loose because sea conditions decide the schedule. October to May is the most reliable window.

## How the islands fit together

The Andamans are an island chain in the Bay of Bengal, and tourism is concentrated in a few places. Port Blair is the capital and arrival point. Havelock, officially Swaraj Dweep, is the main beach and diving base. Neil Island, or Shaheed Dweep, is smaller, calmer and good for a night or two of slow time.

Most first-timers follow a simple order: Port Blair, Havelock, Neil, then back to Port Blair for the flight home. Additional areas such as Baratang, Ross Island and Diglipur can be added when time allows.

### Getting there and permits

Flights reach Port Blair from Chennai, Kolkata and other major cities, with connections from Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi. Flying from Chennai is often the most direct option. Always check current schedules and baggage rules.

Carry valid photo ID for ferry bookings and entry points. Some areas of the islands require permits or are restricted, and entry rules can change, so check the current rules with the official authorities or with us before you travel.

## Port Blair: history and a first sunset

Port Blair is a gateway rather than a main attraction, but it has one essential visit: the Cellular Jail. Its light and sound show in the evening tells the story of the independence movement and is moving for many visitors. Check current show timings and language options in advance.

![Cellular Jail Port Blair](/photos/places/andaman-and-nicobar/cellular-jail-port-blair.jpg)

Other short options include Ross Island, with its colonial-era ruins, and the sunset at Chidiya Tapu. Corbyn's Cove is the nearest easy beach.

## Havelock: beaches and the sea

Havelock is the heart of most trips. Radhanagar Beach is famous for its long white sweep of sand and calm evening light. Elephant Beach is known for snorkelling and water sports, usually reached by a short boat ride or a forest walk when conditions permit.

![Scuba Diving Havelock](/photos/places/andaman-and-nicobar/scuba-diving-havelock.jpg)

If you want to try scuba diving, Havelock has several certified dive operators and beginner programmes called discover scuba. Ask about safety briefings, instructor ratios and equipment standards, and never be pressured into a dive if you feel unwell or anxious. Check for medical conditions that might restrict diving.

> **Tip:** Book ferries and activities early in peak months, and keep your last day as a buffer. Weather and sea conditions can delay or cancel boats, and a tight connection to your flight is the most common cause of stress.

## Neil Island: slow time

Neil Island is smaller and quieter than Havelock. Bharatpur Beach is good for shallow snorkelling and glass-bottom boat rides, while Laxmanpur Beach is a favourite for sunsets. The Natural Bridge, a rock arch formed by the sea, is best seen at low tide. Tides decide the timing, so ask your hotel for the day's schedule.

![Laxmanpur Sunset Neil](/photos/places/andaman-and-nicobar/laxmanpur-sunset-neil.jpg)

A night or two is plenty. Rent a bicycle or use local autos to get around, and enjoy simple seafood meals at small restaurants.

## Ferries and getting around

Both government and private ferries link Port Blair, Havelock and Neil. Private operators are usually faster and more comfortable, while the government boats are cheaper and less frequent. Book in advance, carry ID, and arrive at the jetty on time. Sea conditions can change schedules at short notice.

On each island, taxis, autos and rented scooters are common. Roads are small, and distances are short.

## Best time to visit

- **October to May:** Generally the most comfortable season, with calmer seas and better visibility for snorkelling.
- **December to February:** Peak season, with higher rates and busier ferries.
- **June to September:** Monsoon, with heavy rain, rougher seas and fewer activities. Some water sports close, but it is lush and quiet.

Check the current season and forecasts before you commit. For a wider look at timing across the country, see our [month-by-month guide to India](/blog/best-time-to-visit-india-month-by-month).

### Practical tips

- Carry cash, as ATMs can be limited and card acceptance is patchy outside main areas.
- Mobile networks can be slow, and some islands have weak coverage.
- Use reef-friendly sunscreen, and do not touch or take coral or shells.
- Pack light clothes, a rain jacket, sandals, a hat and a small dry bag for boat rides.
- Alcohol and plastics rules differ by island, so check locally.
