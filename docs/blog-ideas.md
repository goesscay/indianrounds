# Blog ideas for Indian Rounds

The blog should help a traveller decide **where to go**, **when**, **how long** and **how to plan it**, then point them to the right Indian Rounds trip. Every idea below fits one of those jobs. Write each article as a file in `apps/api/database/data/blog/` (format in [blog-content-format.md](blog-content-format.md)) and load it with `php artisan blog:seed-posts`, or write it in the admin under Posts.

## How to prioritise

1. **Write all "A" ideas first.** They match what travellers search before they book and link straight to a trip page.
2. **Pair each destination guide with one food or shopping article** for the same place. They get linked from the guide and keep readers on the site.
3. **Refresh the evergreen guides each year**, especially anything about seasons, permits, visas and prices. Every article ends with a reminder to check current rules.
4. **Use real photos**: each article needs a cover and two or three photos from `apps/web/public/photos`. Add new photos to the folder and credit them in `docs/photo-credits.md`.
5. **Suggested pace:** two articles a week keeps the blog fresh. Set `age: 0` in the file to publish today.
